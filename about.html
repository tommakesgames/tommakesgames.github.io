    About

    I'm a video games researcher, educator, speaker and events organiser based in London, UK.

    With regards to education I've been at this a while: 7 years as a high-school (11-18 year olds) science teacher, couple of years as a TEFL teacher (Teaching English as a Foreign Language), then teaching in Higher Education for over 10 years since 2015 - including significant experience with online learning using Coursera and degree management/development.

    Currently Senior Lecturer in Games Development at University of Greenwich. Previously at Goldsmiths, University of London and University for the Creative Arts (UCA).

    My research focuses on how we can design for the mixed-affect and self-reflective emotional experience in video games - the kind of experience we get where we stop to question life, our values, our position in the world and/or our relation to the world around us. I'm primarily interested in how we achieve this using the aspects that are unique to games - namely mechanics, rules, systems and control schemes. 

    As an events organiser I was previously Conference Director at AdventureX - 'The Narrative Games Convention' from 2016 - 2023, where I grew the event from under 100 people to an international sold out event of over 650. I also regularly organise IRL game jams in London.

    PhD in Computing (Goldsmiths), MA in Games Design (Brunel), BSc in Biological Sciences with Industrial Experience (Manchester).
